KALM-NTG ARCHITECTS is a multidisciplinary practice established in 2024, bringing professional services, construction and documentation together across South Africa, Botswana and Zimbabwe.

The practice

Design • Build • Supervise

Our approach connects architectural thinking with practical delivery — from the initial brief and concept through design development, documentation, procurement, construction, contract administration and project close-out.

The company profile describes KALM.Ntg as a multidisciplinary practice with Architectural, Civil & Structural, Quantity Surveying and Project Management capabilities, supported by strategic partnerships with other built-environment professionals where required.

The practice positions itself around design innovation, sound management and environmental care, with a commitment to developing solutions appropriate to client requirements and applicable building regulations.

The public-facing brand is KALM-NTG ARCHITECTS; the legal company name is KALM.Ntg Architects (Pty) Ltd.

Methodology & project approach

A structured route from inception to close-out.

Our project approach is built around design excellence, design innovation, sound project management and environmental care. The methodology below follows the sequence described in the company profile.

01

Inception

Briefing, project initiation, preliminary scope development and pre-feasibility assessment.

02

Concept & Viability

Conceptual designs, preliminary cost estimates and a project implementation plan are coordinated for client approval.

03

Detailed Design

The approved concept is developed in sufficient detail to define construction methods, with final design and tender documentation prepared for approval.

04

Documentation & Procurement

Construction documentation is coordinated with other consultants and plan approval is sought from local authorities.

05

Contract Administration & Inspection

Tenders, contractor documentation, site handover, programme, cash flow, progress certificates and quality records are monitored.

06

Project Close-Out

Completion requirements are coordinated, including works completion certification, as-built drawings and close-out reporting.

How we manage delivery

Quality, cost, communication and compliance.

The company profile also sets out supporting management principles used throughout project implementation.

Cost Management

Cost estimation, budgeting and cost control are considered from the early stages, with the Quantity Surveyor playing a key role.

Quality Management

Quality management systems, quality control plans, communication channels, documentation management and human-resource management support consistent delivery.

Communication

The Lead Consultant is responsible for client communication, while the professional team communicates on project matters with the Project Manager copied on correspondence.

Occupational Health & Safety

OHS planning includes contractor-plan review, monitoring, monthly audits, risk assessments and safe-work procedures for specialised activities.
