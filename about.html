KALM-NTG ARCHITECTS is a multidisciplinary practice established in 2024, bringing professional services, construction and documentation together across South Africa, Botswana and Zimbabwe.

The practice

Design • Build • Supervise

Our approach connects architectural thinking with practical delivery — from the initial brief and concept through documentation, approvals, construction and professional supervision.
