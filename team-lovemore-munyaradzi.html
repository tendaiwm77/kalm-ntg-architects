Profile

Architectural leadership grounded in experience.

Lovemore is a holder of a Bachelor of Architecture degree from the National University of Science and Technology in Zimbabwe and is a registered architect with SACAP.

He initially worked as a technician for over 10 years, gaining experience across commercial, industrial, health and residential projects. His work included design development, production of working drawings and site supervision.

He attained his Bachelor of Architecture degree in 2008 and has worked as an architect since then, leading project teams and applying his experience to establish strong conceptual foundations and carry projects through construction and completion.

KALM-NTG

Building the practice.

Lovemore was instrumental in forming KALM.Ntg Architects (Pty) Ltd in 2012. The company has grown into a multidisciplinary practice bringing architectural, engineering, quantity surveying, project management, construction and related professional capabilities together.

His role combines architectural leadership with project coordination and a practical focus on translating design intent into completed work.
