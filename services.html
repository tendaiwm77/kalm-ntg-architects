Professional delivery

Integrated expertise around the project.

The company profile describes KALM.Ntg as a multidisciplinary practice comprising Architectural, Civil & Structural, Quantity Surveying and Project Management capabilities, with strategic partnerships supporting a full complement of services.

Quantity Surveying

Experienced Quantity Surveyors assist clients in making informed decisions around cost efficiency during design and project implementation, with cost planning, budgeting and control forming part of the delivery process.

Civil & Structural Engineering

Engineering capability includes design and site supervision for structural steel and reinforced concrete buildings, high- and low-rise buildings, water and wastewater structures, warehouses and sporting facilities.

Project Management

Project and programme management keeps work coordinated and on schedule through risk analysis, design review, professional-team coordination, programming and quality control.

Occupational Health & Safety

Health and safety services support compliance through project specifications, contractor-plan review, monitoring and regular OHS audits in line with applicable requirements.

Construction Supervision

Site-level supervision and contract administration support monitoring of programme, cash flow, documentation, progress certificates, testing and completion requirements.

Cost Management

Cost management is integrated from the early stages through cost estimation, budgeting and cost control, with clear design information helping reduce avoidable cost overruns.
