Website privacy.

How information submitted through this website is intended to be handled.

When you contact KALM-NTG through the website, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, project location and project details. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the services you request.

Communication

The website's enquiry form prepares an email for you to send to kalmntgarch@gmail.com. Information is therefore transmitted through your email provider when you send the message.

Third-party services

The website may link to Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and other external services. Their own privacy policies apply when you use those services.

Questions
